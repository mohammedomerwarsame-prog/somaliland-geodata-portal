About the portal

A single, trusted place to find official geospatial data.

Who can do what

VisitorsBrowse the catalog and map, and download open datasets. No account needed.
Registered usersEverything above, plus datasets marked "registered users only".
GIS specialistsStaff of government institutions, approved by an administrator. They upload data on behalf of their institution.
Data reviewersCheck every upload (file, metadata, licence, personal data) and approve or return it. Nothing is published without review.

How data gets published

  1. A specialist uploads a file and fills in the metadata.
  2. The dataset waits in the review queue; it is not public yet.
  3. A reviewer approves it (published) or requests changes (returned to the specialist).
