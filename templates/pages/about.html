About the portal

A single, trusted place to find official geospatial data.

Who can do what

VisitorsBrowse the catalog and map, and download open datasets. No account needed.
Registered usersEverything above, plus datasets marked "registered users only".
Portal teamStaff of {{ PORTAL_ORGANIZATION }}. Only the team uploads data, including data sent to us by other institutions.
Team reviewersCheck every upload (file, description, licence, personal data) and approve or return it. Nothing is published without review.

How data gets published

Institutions that want to publish data send it to our team on Telegram or email.

  1. A team member uploads the file and describes it, crediting the source institution.
  2. The dataset waits in the review queue; it is not public yet.
  3. A reviewer approves it (published) or requests changes (returned to the specialist).
