What to send

  1. The data file: a zipped shapefile (.shp, .shx, .dbf, .prj), GeoJSON, GeoPackage, GeoTIFF, KML or CSV with coordinates.
  2. A short description: what the data shows, which area it covers, and when it was collected.
  3. The source: your institution's name, and who produced the data.
  4. Permission: confirm that the data can be shared publicly, or tell us if it should be for registered users only.
Please remove personal information (names, phone numbers, ID numbers) before sending.
